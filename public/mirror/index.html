How it Works

  1. Wastewater gravity flows to a septic tank where settlement occurs.
  2. Effluent gravity flows into AES pipes in a sand bed.
  3. The vented, fabric-wrapped AES pipes provide a living environment for microbes to break down effluent to a safe level.
  4. After filtering through the pipes and sand, high quality effluent disperses into the soil.

Why Choose AES

Your guarantee of quality on all AES components

AES is suitable for all projects large and small; houses, holiday homes, cafés, motels, seasonal worker accommodation, campgrounds and subdivisions.

It achieves very high treatment levels for your wastewater. Third-party tested in New Zealand and internationally, the AES system tested at the National Testing Facility (OSET-NTP) in Rotorua reduced contaminants to one tenth of the NZ standard for secondary treatment.

AES uses simple technology with no pumps, alarms. Just the septic tank pump-out when required, about every 5-7 years that most wastewater systems require. It’s the environmentally sustainable system that you can trust to keep your family and environment safe from contaminants, and saves you thousands over its lifetime.

With AES, homeowners avoid the hassles of alarms, noisy or failing pumps, filters to clean or sewage overflow. Using proven US technology AES is installed worldwide.

Step 3: Installation

With an approved consent your system can be installed by an AES certified drainlayer/plumber. 
If your preferred drainlayer is not a certified AES installer, they can do our free online training course.
Or, call us and we will arrange for a price or prices for you from the AES Plumber / Drainlayer installers in your area. Contact Us.
The installer will arrange the purchase and delivery of AES components and the supply of a locally produced septic tank then  assemble your wastewater treatment system.
Our 20 year warranty on the AES components also gives you peace of mind that no other system offers.

Step 2: Design & Consent

The AES designer will visit your site and in conjunction with you determine the best placement of the system in relation to property, boundaries, water sources, your soil category, underground water table etc in accordance with NZ standards and your local Council rules.
From this onsite assessment the AES Designer will create a Design Report to be included with your application for building consent.
As part of this process Environment Technology will peer review your AES design for your additional security.

Step 1: Contact Us

Our team can answer any questions you have about the AES Combined Treatment and Dispersal System and how it will fit to your site. We can give you a ballpark cost assessment and organise for an onsite assessment for a wastewater design by one of our accredited AES designers in your area.
